# #46: Breath 478

A hyper-minimalist 4-7-8 breathing guide for instant relaxation. No distractions, no ads, just a simple tool to reset your nervous system.

## 1 Tool / 1 Action
- **Start / Stop**: One tap to begin the scientifically-backed 4-7-8 breathing cycle (Inhale 4s, Hold 7s, Exhale 8s).
- **Visual Guide**: A pulsing #333 circle with a soft green glow guides your rhythm without needing to look at a timer.

## Core Philosophy
- **Performance**: Lightweight and fast, optimized for low-spec mobile and PC browsers.
- **Privacy**: No login / No tracking / No ads / Browser only.
- **Design**: Fridge Combo Signature UI (White background, #333 elements, 15px border-radius).

## How to use
1. Tap **START**.
2. Follow the 3-second preparation countdown.
3. Breathe according to the visual expansion (Inhale), pause (Hold), and contraction (Exhale).
4. Tap **STOP** anytime to reset.
